\section{Research question}

Holding fixture, frozen sample, encoding, \textbf{cautious} semantics, nd
folding, and all other learner options constant: how does changing
\texttt{asm\_intro(relto)} to \texttt{asm\_intro(sechk)} affect what ABALearn
learns and how search proceeds under cautious nd?

\section{Main finding --- contrast on child $c$}

\subsection{They diverge}

Fixture~1 $c$ and Fixture~2 $c$: both arms \texttt{solved}; deltas differ;
inputs byte-identical within pairs.

\subsection{Why}

After failed post-fold entailment:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Relto:} relative-first; often \texttt{KO} under cautious
    \texttt{gen3}; backtrack to another fold literal.
  \item \textbf{Sechk:} always mint provisional $\alpha$; \texttt{gen5}/stable
    extension; often still reaches $b$/$d$, but later / via deeper nests.
\end{itemize}
First fork:
\begin{verbatim}
relto:  looking for assumption relative to
sechk:  generating NEW assumption: alpha_1(A)
\end{verbatim}
H7a's brave first-BK latch does \emph{not} describe these cautious-$c$ sechk
deltas.

\subsection{What it looks like}

\textbf{Q1:} same $a$-gate; sechk inserts $\alpha_2$ nest then closes with
\texttt{b\_val\_1} (relto closes with \texttt{b\_val\_0}).
\textbf{Q2:} both cite $a,b,d$; sechk thicker (5 vs 3 asms; $\sim$899 vs
$\sim$257 lines).
