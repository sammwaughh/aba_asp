how do permitted \texttt{folding\_steps}$(M)$ (H6a) and nested sample size $n$

\section{Main findings}

\subsection{H6a --- folding-steps ablation}

On frozen \texttt{n30\_seed42}, for roots $a$/$b$, \texttt{prolog.stdout} length
grows roughly linearly with $M\in\{1,2,5,10\}$
($\sim$549 $\to$ 1047 $\to$ 2541 $\to$ 5031 lines for $a$; mirrored for $b$).
Outcome stays \texttt{completed\_no\_solution} at every $M$.
Control $c$ solves inside \texttt{tokens}$(1)$ with identical delta and
unchanged 134-line / $\sim$1.3s trace at all four budgets.

\textbf{Why predictable.}
\texttt{folding\_steps}$(M)$ does \emph{not} mean ``perform exactly $M$ folds.''
Under nd, learning starts at \texttt{tokens}$(1)$.
When a full attempt under the current token budget fails, \texttt{gen.pl} may
print \texttt{* Increasing folding tokens to: T1} and retry while $T_1\le M$.
On these roots, each budget replays essentially the same failed cautious motif
($\sim$9 NEW assumptions / $\sim$500-line band).
Higher $M$ multiplies failed-band replays:
$\mathrm{lines}\approx C_{\mathrm{fixed}}+M\cdot C_{\mathrm{band}}$.
There is no successful $M^*$ through $M{=}10$ --- raising $M$ does not buy a
new theory; it only replays failure (H4 wasted headroom).

\subsection{H6b --- nested sample-size ablation}

At fixed \texttt{folding\_steps}$(2)$, for roots $a$/$b$, trace length grows
roughly with $n\in\{30,60,90\}$ ($\sim$1047 $\to$ 1641 $\to$ 2241 lines for $a$).
Search topology is fixed (NEW${=}18$, KO${=}32$; one token increase to 2).
What grows is per-band bookkeeping (more grounded $E^\pm$ ids; larger
rote/\texttt{ert} and subsumption sweeps; larger contrary batches).

\textbf{Why predictable.}
With motif $\times$ budget structure held fixed, cost per band should scale
roughly with grounding / example size $\Rightarrow$ total length roughly
$\propto n$ in this limited range.
Support \emph{presence} of the four atoms is already complete at $n{=}30$;
multiplicities and computational grounding size change.
H6b is not ``pure compute with every statistical property fixed.''
Line ratios are near-linear; runtime slightly more superlinear.
Do not claim a universal complexity law.

\subsection{Cross lead}

H4's long root traces ($\sim$5k lines / $\sim$66--70s at $n30\times$steps10)
are primarily cumulative nd token-budget replays of one cautiously failed motif
(H6a), with a secondary per-band cost that grows with $n$ at fixed $M{=}2$
(H6b); control $c$ stays solved and delta-stable; nothing here is root
mechanism recovery or an $n\times$steps interaction result.
