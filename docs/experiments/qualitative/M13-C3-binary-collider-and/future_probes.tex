Status: \textbf{run / analysed} / not a claim.

\textbf{Evaluative stance (locked for H1 reading).}

H0 AAMAS root \texttt{completed\_no\_solution} is the \textbf{desired / correct}

H1 AAMAS root \texttt{solved} with co-occurrence Horn rules is an

\subsection{H1a --- spurious root rule under missing negative witness}

On this fixture, AAMAS, target \(a\) (symmetrically \(b\)), seed~42: the nested
prefix \(n{=}25\) \textbf{omits all \((0,0,0)\) rows}.
Learning \textbf{solved} with a delta that includes the both-0 co-occurrence
rule
\begin{verbatim}
a(A) :- b_val_0(A), c_val_0(A).
\end{verbatim}
(respectively \texttt{b(A) :- a\_val\_0(A), c\_val\_0(A).}), which is
\textbf{false} as a population implication and is blocked on the closed H0
\(n{=}30\) table by \(E^-\) rows~26 and~30.

Nested-prefix fact (seed~42): rows~1--25 contain no \((0,0,0)\) atom;
rows~26 and~30 are the only \((0,0,0)\) observations in \(n{=}30\).
Verified: \texttt{n25\_seed42.csv} is a row-for-row prefix of
\texttt{n30\_seed42.csv}.

\subsection{H1b --- negative witness in the unsafe cell}

If the sample contains at least one opposite-label row in that both-0 predictor
cell, the both-0 rule fails brave entailment under the observed AAMAS path. On
closed H0 \(n{=}30\) this manifested as
\textbf{\texttt{completed\_no\_solution}}, not as emission of only the safe
both-1 rule.
That H0 outcome is the \textbf{correct} reading for roots under the H1 stance.

\subsection{Explicit non-claims for H1}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Full support does \textbf{not} imply AAMAS will emit a ``correct''
    deterministic root mechanism rule: roots have
    \texttt{no\_observed\_parent\_deterministic\_rule} in the evaluator
    reference.
  \item ``One of each population atom'' is a sufficient practical condition on
    this approved sample, but the operational point is a \textbf{label conflict
    inside the greedy candidate body cell}, not a generic demand for full joint
    support.
  \item H1 does not claim that omitting \((0,0,0)\) is the only way AAMAS can
    emit a spurious root rule, nor that every seed behaves identically.
  \item H1 does \textbf{not} claim that root \texttt{solved} on \(n{=}25\) is
    progress or mechanism recovery.
\end{itemize}
