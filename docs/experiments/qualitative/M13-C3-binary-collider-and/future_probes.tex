\subsection{H1a --- spurious root rule under missing negative witness}

On this fixture, AAMAS, target \(a\) (symmetrically \(b\)), seed~42: the nested
prefix \(n{=}25\) \textbf{omits all \((0,0,0)\) rows}.
Learning \textbf{solved} with a delta that includes the both-0 co-occurrence

\begin{verbatim}

\end{verbatim}
(respectively \texttt{b(A) :- a\_val\_0(A), c\_val\_0(A).}), which is
\textbf{false} as a population implication and is blocked on the closed H0
\(n{=}30\) table by \(E^-\) rows~26 and~30.

Nested-prefix fact (seed~42): rows~1--25 contain no \((0,0,0)\) atom;
rows~26 and~30 are the only \((0,0,0)\) observations in \(n{=}30\).
Verified: \texttt{n25\_seed42.csv} is a row-for-row prefix of
\texttt{n30\_seed42.csv}.

\subsection{H1b --- negative witness in the unsafe cell}

If the sample contains at least one opposite-label row in that both-0 predictor
cell, the both-0 rule fails brave entailment under the observed AAMAS path. On
closed H0 \(n{=}30\) this manifested as
\textbf{\texttt{completed\_no\_solution}}, not as emission of only the safe
both-1 rule.
That H0 outcome is the \textbf{correct} reading for roots under the H1 stance.

On ECAI \textbf{brave} (\texttt{configs/ecai2024\_config.pl}:

Rows~9 and~26 share identical BK literals \texttt{b\_val\_0}, \texttt{c\_val\_0}.
No deterministic rule over \((B,C)\) assigns both labels. The brave delta for
\(a\) includes the \(\alpha_2\)/\(\alpha_3\) nest (verified in
\texttt{delta.aba}):
\begin{verbatim}
a(A) :- alpha_2(A), b_val_0(A).
c_alpha_2(A) :- alpha_3(A), c_val_0(A).
c_alpha_3(A) :- alpha_2(A), b_val_0(A).
\end{verbatim}

Trace/fixture integration reading (for the signpost): this is a \textbf{per-row
assumption choice gadget} --- one stable model can make the target hold at
row~9 and reject it at row~26 because ground assumption atoms differ. Runner \texttt{solved} means brave
coverage of \(E^+\)/\(E^-\), \textbf{not} recovery of a functional root
mechanism (\texttt{no\_observed\_parent\_deterministic\_rule}).

Engine fact (\texttt{asp\_engine.pl}, \texttt{entails/5}): brave entailment
requires existence of a stable model under joint \(E^+\)/\(E^-\) constraints;
cautious entailment requires \(E^+\) atoms to lie in \textbf{cautious
consequences} (true in every answer set) and \(E^-\) atoms not to. Under that
reading, the current brave delta should \textbf{fail} cautious entailment:
e.g.\ \(a(9)\) is not true in every stable model of the choice gadget.
Cautious re-check after contrary rote is invoked in \texttt{gen.pl}
(\texttt{gen6}).

(\textbf{required before H4 can run; not yet done})}

\textbf{target-wise} path currently \textbf{hard-requires brave}.

\texttt{\_read\_learning\_mode}): the runner reads
\texttt{set\_lopt(learning\_mode(\ldots))} from the Prolog config and
\textbf{rejects} any mode other than \texttt{brave}, with an error that the

states that the supported regime selects brave learning. Existing configs only
supply \texttt{learner.prolog\_config} (e.g.\ \texttt{configs/ecai2024\_config.pl});
learning mode is \textbf{not} a free YAML field --- it is derived from the
consulted \texttt{.pl} file and then validated.
