This document inspects each experimental cell

\[
(\text{fixture},\ \text{target }t\in N,\ \text{config}\in\{\text{ECAI},\text{AAMAS}\})
\]

in plain English. Prefer **config** (not “arm”). The object of study is unguided ABA
Learning **rule recovery**, not parent-set scoring and not Russo-style Causal ABA.

For every cell: (i) what was intended (\(\mathcal{D}\), \(E^\pm\), \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\),
semantic success); (ii) what was learned (target δ); (iii) whether it matches
\(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\) / the semantic description; (iv) what was recovered instead.

Judgment uses the Approach’s inspection-first criterion:

> an intensional, general rule that correctly characterises \(t\neq 0\) on \(\mathcal{D}\),
> using ancestor information appropriately, without rote sample-id casework and without
> citing descendants.

Compact `nz` \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\) is preferred; an equivalent `val`-expansion is
**inferior**. Citing a **descendant** is an **explicit failure mode**. Citing a
sibling / isolated non-ancestor is a **divergence to inspect**.

ASP `pos_covered` / `neg_rejected` (from the summary matrix) show sample coverage; they
are not a substitute for intensional match to \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\).

# Batch A — ECAI config (`M12x_ecai2024`)

## A1. ECAI × U1 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** \(G\): \(x_1\to x_2\), \(x_0\) isolated. \(x_2:=\operatorname{copy}(x_1)\).
Ancestors \(\{x_1\}\); other distractor \(\{x_0\}\); no descendants.
\(\mathcal{H}_{x_2}^\star\): `x2(A) :- x1_nz(A).`
Semantic success: characterise \(x_2\neq 0\) via the unique parent, without citing \(x_0\).

**Learned.**

```prolog
x2(A) :- alpha_1(A), x0_val_0(A).
x2(A) :- alpha_2(A), x0_val_1(A).
x2(A) :- alpha_3(A), x0_val_2(A).
```

(Contraries on the alphas encode the true parent’s zero values.)

**Match?** No. Not compact `nz`; cites the **isolated distractor** \(x_0\) in every
target head rule; parent information appears mainly in contraries.

**Instead.** Assumption-mediated partition on \(x_0\), with the true ancestor recovered
only as contrary conditions. Extensionally covers \(E^\pm\) on \(\mathcal{D}\)
(`6/6`, `3/3`) but fails the intended reading.

## A2. ECAI × U2 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** Collider; \(x_2:=\min(x_0,x_1)\). Ancestors \(\{x_0,x_1\}\).
\(\mathcal{H}_{x_2}^\star\): `x2(A) :- x0_nz(A), x1_nz(A).`
Semantic success: conjunctive / AND nonzero rule.

**Learned.**

```prolog
x2(A) :- alpha_1(A), x0_val_1(A).
x2(A) :- alpha_2(A), x0_val_2(A).
```

**Match?** No. Not the compact AND of `*_nz`. Body-scope cites only \(x_0\); the
second parent is encoded via assumptions/contraries.

**Instead.** Assumption-mediated casework on one ancestor. Extensionally adequate
(`4/4`, `5/5`); intensionally not \(\mathcal{H}^\star\).

## A3. ECAI × U3 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** Same collider; \(x_2:=\max\).
\(\mathcal{H}_{x_2}^\star\): `x2(A) :- x0_nz(A).` and `x2(A) :- x1_nz(A).`

**Learned.**

```prolog
x2(A) :- alpha_1(A), x0_val_0(A).
x2(A) :- x0_val_1(A).
x2(A) :- x0_val_2(A).
```

**Match?** No. Covers “\(x_0\) nonzero” by `val` and the \(x_0=0\) slice by an
assumption (with contraries supplying the other parent). Not the two-rule OR of `*_nz`.

**Instead.** One-sided OR on \(x_0\) plus assumption repair. Inferior to
\(\mathcal{H}^\star\); does not cite \(x_1\) in undefeated target bodies.

# Batch B — AAMAS config (`M12x_aamas2025`)

## B1. AAMAS × U1 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** As A1: \(\mathcal{H}_{x_2}^\star\): `x2(A) :- x1_nz(A).`; do not cite \(x_0\).

**Learned.** Six conjunctive rules mixing `x0_val_*` / `x0_nz` with `x1_nz` and
`x1_val_*` (full DNF-style covering of the six positives).

**Match?** No. Uses the true parent’s `nz`/`val` **and** the **isolated distractor**
\(x_0\) in every rule.

**Instead.** Over-specific casework that is extensionally correct but cites a
non-ancestor distractor. Not compact \(\mathcal{H}^\star\).

---

## B2. AAMAS × U2 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** \(\mathcal{H}_{x_2}^\star\): AND of `x0_nz`, `x1_nz`.

**Learned.** Four rules: all pairs of nonzero values for \((x_0,x_1)\) with both
`*_nz` and matching `*_val_*`.

**Match?** No exact compact `nz` conjunction. Semantically this is a `val`-blown
enumeration of the AND region (inferior / not \(\mathcal{H}^\star\)).

**Instead.** Dense DNF over the positive nonzero rectangle; ancestors only (no
descendant on this sink). Closer in spirit to AND than ECAI’s assumption encoding,
but not the preferred compact form.

---

## B3. AAMAS × U3 × \(t=x_2\)

**Intended.** OR of `x0_nz`, `x1_nz`.

**Learned.** Eight rules enumerating the nonzero OR region (including mixed zero /
nonzero cases).

**Match?** No. Not the two-rule compact OR.

**Instead.** Full DNF casework for \(\Phi_{x_2}=z_0\lor z_1\). Extensionally fine;
intensionally far from \(\mathcal{H}^\star\).

the sibling.

## Cross-config patterns (input to M1.3)

These are induced observations, not a forced taxonomy.

1. **No exact compact-`nz` \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\)** on this 22-cell grid under either
   published config.
2. **ECAI** often prefers **assumption introduction** and first folds toward an early
   BK block (`x0_*`), recovering other parents mainly as contraries; on U4/U5-x1 it
   produces clean (but inferior) parent `val`-expansions and **does not** cite
   descendants/siblings in those cells.
3. **AAMAS** produces denser **`val`+`nz` casework**. It more often **keeps distractors
   that are extensionally correlated**: siblings (U4), and especially **descendants**
   (U5-x1, U6-x2, U7-x2) — the Approach’s explicit failure mode.
4. **Parent vs ancestor (U5-x2):** ECAI uses grandparent only; AAMAS uses both.
   Neither returns preferred compact `x1_nz`.
5. **ASP coverage is not the bottleneck:** every cell covers \(E^+\) and rejects \(E^-\)
   on \(\mathcal{D}\). Divergence is intensional / structural relative to
   \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\) and the semantic success description.
