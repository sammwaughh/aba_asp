## Purpose

Run the locked expanded M1.2 design (Approach + unit set + mechanism cards) as a
22-cell grid: 7 deterministic units × non-source targets × {ECAI, AAMAS}, with
nonzero-positive labels and definitional `*_nz` BK (descendants kept in BK as
distractors).

## Research question

> When and how do the ECAI and AAMAS published ABA Learning configurations recover
> correct, general, intensional rules from deterministic categorical mechanisms
> (copy/min/max; full source factorial; nonzero-positive), and what do they recover
> instead?

## Theoretical motivation

- **ABA foundations / ABA Learning:** unguided rule recovery under two published configs.
- **Causal ABA:** not exercised.
- **Current bridge:** target-wise learned-rule recovery over tabular BK + E±.

## Relation to ABA Learning

- BK: `xi_val_v` + definitional `xi_nz` facts for every non-target column (including
  descendants).
- \(E^+=\{t\neq 0\}\), \(E^-=\{t=0\}\).
- Configs: `configs/ecai2024_config.pl`, `configs/aamas2025_config.pl`.

## Relation to Causal ABA

Does **not** test Russo-style Causal ABA.
