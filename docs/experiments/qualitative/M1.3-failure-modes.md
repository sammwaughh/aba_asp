## Research question

> Across the expanded M1.2 graphs and DGP(s), when does unguided ABA Learning recover
> the intended mechanism-aligned rules, what does it recover instead when it does not,
> and what evidence explains those patterns?

- **Current bridge:** target-wise learned-rule recovery; ASP coverage kept separate
  from intended-rule match.

  26 final target rules through accepted generalisations and subsumption.

  `x1_val_0` as its body.
- AAMAS invariant audit: in 11/11 cells, target-rule count equals \(|E^+|\);
  each rule retains the complete non-target variable scope; no `nz` appears without
  its corresponding nonzero exact-value literal; assumptions = 0.
- ECAI ND audit: no token-increase landmark in any cell; entailment-relative
  subsumption occurs in the seven assumption-bearing cells.
- AAMAS MGR audit: one precomputation phase per cell; no `is more general than`
  deletion landmark; actual greedy-fold count equals \(|E^+|\).

## Interpretation

The initial pass changes the emphasis from exact \(\mathcal{H}_t^\star\) matching to
specific learned representations and transformation paths. In particular, ECAI
conditions can be split between target rules and contraries, while AAMAS produces
row-specific full-predictor descriptions. These remain observations to test, not final
mechanistic conclusions.
