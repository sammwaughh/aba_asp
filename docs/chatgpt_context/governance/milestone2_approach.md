# Milestone 2 — Causal ABA and ABA Learning integration

**Status:** open. The approach is intentionally provisional.

## Purpose

Milestone 2 concerns the relationship between argumentative causal discovery and ABA
Learning. Its immediate priority is to develop an intimate working knowledge of both:

- the argumentative causal discovery theory presented in the Causal ABA paper; and
- the corresponding ArgCausalDisco code and its executable behaviour.

This requires close study of the paper and code rather than relying on summaries,
terminological similarity, or assumptions about how the implementation works. The
relationship between the formal account and the implementation should be understood well
enough to explain what the system represents, what it computes, how it computes it, and
what its outputs mean.

Once that understanding has been developed, possible forms of integration between
argumentative causal discovery and ABA Learning should be considered, tried, and tested.
The completed Milestone-1 evidence may inform this work, but it does not determine the
form that an integration should take.

## Current boundary

No integration architecture or experimental approach is fixed at the opening of this
milestone. In particular, this document does not prescribe:

- how ArgCausalDisco and ABA Learning should interact;
- which system should guide, constrain, call, or supply information to the other;
- what an integrated system should take as input or produce as output;
- which representations, semantics, algorithms, fixtures, metrics, or comparisons should
  be used; or
- how many investigations should be conducted or in what order.

Those questions should be addressed only after the paper and ArgCausalDisco code are
understood sufficiently well. The subsequent direction will be decided by Samuel with his
supervisors, informed by what that investigation reveals and by the results of attempted
integrations.

Milestone 1 remains closed. Its evidence is preserved, with the Bucket-3 synthesis in
`docs/experiments/qualitative/M13-C3-binary-collider-and/findings_for_fabrizio.tex`.
