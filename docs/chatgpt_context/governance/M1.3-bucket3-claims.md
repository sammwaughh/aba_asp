## Supervisor-defined scope at opening (historical)

The opening planning process considered:

- controlled variation of the learning target and movement towards target-agnostic
  analysis of when target-wise outputs support recovery of causal structure;
- controlled variation of data availability, with the exact meaning of missing data
  (rows, cell values, variables, or combinations) still to be decided;
- larger graph fixtures, motivated in part by the four-node U7 diamond;
- explicit graph–mechanism validity and clear statements of support;
- explicit analysis of correlation, marginal independence, conditional independence,
  and Markov equivalence classes;
- separation of failures caused by a specified ABA Learning strategy from limitations
  caused by observational equivalence, missing information, representation, or data;
- possible variation in category count and possible non-discrete extensions.

These dimensions were planning possibilities rather than closure requirements. Those not
addressed by H0–H7b are deferred beyond Milestone 1; they are not unfinished Bucket-3 work
and are not automatically part of Milestone 2.

## 31 July investigation direction (completed)

The completed H0–H7b investigation used binary causal models with:

- mutually independent, non-degenerate stochastic root distributions;
- deterministic non-root structural functions;
- a target-free IID table sampled through the roots and deterministic propagation;
- all variables considered separately as learning targets on the same frozen table; and
- a pre-run evaluator-only reference against which learned frameworks can later be
  described.

Future fixtures use lowercase internal identifiers `a`, `b`, `c`, ... for YAML, CSV,
ABA predicates, targets, and paths, while prose and diagrams may use (A,B,C,\ldots).
Existing `xN` fixtures and evidence remain unchanged. ABA learner symbols `alpha_N` and
`c_alpha_N` occupy a separate reserved namespace.

For each fixture, the reference must record:

- root distributions and complete deterministic truth tables;
- formal, population-supported, and finite-sample-observed parent configurations;
- canonical exact-value positive-state rules where the current binary encoding applies;
- that a root target has no deterministic causal rule over other observed variables; and
- that semantic equivalence cannot be reduced to exact rule-string equality.

The generating graph, population certificate, support information, and mechanism
reference are evaluator-only. They must not enter learner-visible BK or examples.

Every fixture still requires an exact population-level audit. Root stochasticity,
deterministic propagation, active edges, and DAG factorisation do not individually or
jointly guarantee ordinary faithfulness.

## Theory point carried forward

For an isolated two-node copy relation (A \to B, B:=A), observational dependence does
not distinguish the generating orientation from (B \to A). Additional variables may
provide orienting information through the larger graph, but this is fixture-dependent and
is not assumed in advance.
